# MedSpace research

## Evidence boundary — live clinic, 3 November 2026

The conservative ETA buffer, daily numbering, 24-hour queue links, three-photo request, two-minute voice-message limit and media quotas are founder/product/security decisions. They are not observed clinic averages or validated clinical protocols. Automated synthetic database, HTTP and simultaneous browser tests establish implementation behavior only. Web Audio supplies a synthetic tone during recording tests; no patient or clinician audio is captured. Physical microphone/device, printer, clinician acceptance and actual ETA accuracy require a real pilot. Existing interview qualifications below remain unchanged.
