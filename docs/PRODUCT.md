# MedSpace product

## Live clinic update — 3 November 2026

The founder authorized completion of the original app's single-doctor live flow: reception check-in/registration and a queue-only QR link; stable daily numbers; live patient position and a conservative, doctor-configured ETA; pause/resume; doctor call/start/return/complete; assigned follow-up photos; and short doctor voice messages with preview and explicit sharing. [LIVE_CLINIC.md](LIVE_CLINIC.md) is the implementation contract. Existing patient charts, intake review, manually entered prescriptions and care plans remain part of the connected visit. Multi-doctor scheduling, billing and research hypotheses below are not silently converted into requirements. Hosted document scanning stays deferred in [FUTURE_WORK.md](FUTURE_WORK.md).
